## Design Quality — 8/10

The section earns an 8 because it makes several choices a template would never make. The stacked single-column layout (`work-card-stack`, `globals.css:735-741`: `flex-direction: column; gap: 0`) is a deliberate rejection of the card grid. Cards abut each other with only a 1px `--color-divider` rule at top; the stack is closed bottom by `border-bottom: 1px solid var(--color-divider)` on the wrapper. That austere bookkeeping reads as a ledger — exactly what a pattern book is.

Typography hierarchy is tight. The category label at `0.66rem / 0.22em letter-spacing` (`.case-card-category`, `globals.css:781-789`) creates the garment-tag fingerprint. Syne 600 title at `1.4rem` (`.case-card-title`, `globals.css:791-796`) provides weight without competing with the section heading. The closer text (`.case-card-closer`, Outfit italic, `0.88rem`, amber-light, `globals.css:822-827`) lands tonally separate — it reads like a maker's annotation in a margin, not a CTA. The card number at `0.55rem / opacity 0.25` (`.case-card-number`, `globals.css:771-779`) reads as a tailor's notation, not a headline — exactly the intended register.

The amber rule hover sweep is structurally correct: `.case-card::before` starts at `width: 0` and transitions to `width: 100%` on hover. The `translateY(-2px)` lift + 6% amber box-shadow (`color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-amber) 6%, transparent)`, `globals.css:762-765`) is well-calibrated — emergence, not decoration.

One concern: `.section-heading` has no `letter-spacing` override beyond the global base. At `clamp(1.8rem, 3.5vw, 2.5rem)` with `margin-top: 1.75rem` after the garment tag (`globals.css:387-393`), the heading sits at default word spacing while everything below it is tracked and composed. The vertical rhythm between tag → heading → card stack is functional but not composed. There is no deliberate breathing room that signals "these three items are a set."

## Originality — 8/10

This section passes the AI Slop Test on every structural point:

1. Cards have zero `border-radius` — `globals.css` has no `border-radius` on `.case-card`. Sharp edges throughout.
2. No drop shadow at rest. Box-shadow only appears on `:hover`, at 6% opacity — a material shift, not decoration (`globals.css:764`).
3. No icons, no "Read more" CTAs. The amber category label is the sole visual anchor.
4. Roman numeral card numbering (I/III, II/III, III/III) at 0.55rem / 25% amber opacity is a choice that would never appear in a portfolio starter kit. It is a tailor's notation applied literally.
5. Metric list density is distinctive. Eleven spec items in flowing inline text, separated by amber dots at `0.4` opacity (`globals.css:816-820`), within `0.78rem / line-height 2` type signals thoroughness in a way generic portfolio cards do not attempt.

The houndstooth texture (`#work::before`, `globals.css:2019-2030`) is a thoughtful SVG data URI: checker half-blocks at `rgba(255,255,255,0.025)` plus polygon teeth at `0.012` and `0.01` — two fill levels that build toward the pattern's characteristic pointed edge, rather than a lazy checkerboard. At this opacity it is genuinely subliminal.

What prevents a 9: the section's distinctiveness is heavily text-dependent. Strip the copy (Roman numerals, metric depth, closer prose) and what remains is a stack of muted-divider horizontal bands with a single amber accent on hover. The form language earns its score through content more than through visual structure alone.

## Craft — 7/10

**What works:**

- Stagger timing is correctly implemented: `.stagger > .fade-in:nth-child(n)` at 0ms / 80ms / 160ms (`globals.css:294-296`). Cards enter at a natural reading cadence.
- The `closer-fade` parent-relative trigger is the best piece of craft in the section. `useScrollReveal` fires `setTimeout(400ms)` when the parent card intersects rather than waiting for the closer's own viewport crossing (`page.tsx:64-68`). This solves a real UX problem on short viewports invisibly.
- The `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` easing is consistent across the fade-in, closer-fade, clip-reveal, tag-enter, and card hover transitions. The section feels choreographed.
- `MetricDot` uses `aria-hidden="true"` and correct inline placement — no additional line height, no screen-reader noise (`page.tsx:26-28`, `globals.css:816-820`).
- Reduced-motion handling covers `.fade-in`, `.closer-fade`, `.clip-reveal`, and `.tag-enter` (`globals.css:1878-1894`). Animations collapse gracefully.

**What prevents an 8:**

- **Amber rule transition is 0.4s, not 0.6s.** The spec explicitly calls for `0.6s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. The implementation at `globals.css:759` uses `0.4s`. The sweep reads slightly rushed on wide viewports where the basting-thread metaphor needs time to register.
- **Hover exit timing mismatch.** On mouseout, `.case-card` retreats via `transform 0.35s` and `box-shadow 0.35s` (`globals.css:747-748`), while `.case-card::before` retreats over `0.4s` (`globals.css:759`). The amber line is visibly the last thing to disappear — a 50ms lag that accumulates across repeated hover interactions. Enter and exit should feel symmetrical.
- **Tag-enter border flicker.** The `::before`/`::after` side rules animate `height: 0 → 100%` in `0.35s` with a `0.15s` delay (`globals.css:258-260`). Against the tag's `0.45s` entrance, the side lines start late and end early — a minor flicker at scroll speeds that breaks the illusion of seamless entry.

## Functionality — 6/10

**What works:**

- Semantic HTML: cards are `<article>` elements with `aria-label` attributes matching their titles (`page.tsx:562, 590, 618`). Section uses `<h2>` → `<h3>` hierarchy — correct.
- `useScrollReveal` threshold (`0.08`) with `rootMargin: "0px 0px -30px 0px"` fires before full card entry without being eager (`page.tsx:72`). Consistent.
- Mobile texture: `@media (max-width: 768px)` sets `#work::before` opacity to `0.5` (`globals.css:2171`). The spec calls for "~50% of desktop value" — this is exactly that.
- Mobile layout: cards remain single-column by default (no flex-direction breakpoint needed). Section padding adjusts via `.section-wrapper` mobile rule.
- Card stack has a bottom border (`globals.css:740`) — the ledger closes visually.

**Issues:**

- **No focus treatment on cards.** Cards are `<article>` elements — non-interactive, no `tabindex`, no `:focus-visible` style. The hover amber rule and lift are invisible to keyboard users. For a portfolio aiming at technical credibility, this is a notable gap.
- **Ruler marks hidden on mobile** (`globals.css:222-226`, `display: none`). The measurement-system dividers that reinforce the brand's precision language disappear on mobile without a fallback. No alternative treatment is provided. The brand reads as less intentional at the breakpoint.
- **No explicit `position: relative` on `#work`.** The `#work::before` pseudo-element requires its parent to establish a positioning context. `#work` inherits `position: relative` and `isolation: isolate` from `.section-wrapper` (which `#work` bears as a class, `globals.css:156-162`) — this works, but it is indirect. If `.section-wrapper` ever gains `position: static`, the texture breaks silently.
- **Card padding is `2rem 0` at all widths.** On viewports below 480px, `2rem` top/bottom padding with no horizontal padding on the card means the card number (`position: absolute; right: 0`) sits flush against the section padding edge. On very narrow screens this could crowd the category label and number into the same horizontal zone.

## Top Issues to Fix

1. **Craft / amber rule at 0.4s, spec requires 0.6s.** `globals.css:759`. Change `transition: width 0.4s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` to `0.6s`. The basting-thread metaphor is the card's signature interaction — it needs the full duration to read as intentional rather than snappy. One-word change, high brand fidelity payoff.

2. **Craft / hover exit timing mismatch.** On mouseout, `.case-card` retreats in `0.35s` while `.case-card::before` retreats in `0.4s` (after fix: `0.6s`). Either bring the `::before` transition back in line with `0.35s` on exit only (using separate transition properties), or extend the card hover transition to `0.5s` so both enter and exit feel symmetrical. Affects `globals.css:747-748` and `759`.

3. **Functionality / no keyboard / focus treatment on cards.** Cards offer no `:focus-visible` state and no `tabindex`. The hover interactions — the amber rule sweep and lift — are entirely invisible to keyboard users. Adding `tabindex="0"` to each `<article>` and a matching `:focus-visible` rule (amber `outline` or the same `::before` sweep triggered by `:focus-visible`) would close the gap without altering the visual design for mouse users.

## What's Working Well

**Design Quality and Originality both scored 8.** The stacked single-column layout, Roman numeral notation, amber rule hover sweep, metric list density, and the houndstooth SVG built with two fill levels are all genuine creative decisions. Do not touch these in iteration.

The `closer-fade` parent-relative trigger (`page.tsx:64-68`) is the most quietly impressive piece of craft in the section. It solves a real short-viewport problem invisibly. This pattern should be preserved and propagated to other sections that use similar delayed reveals.
