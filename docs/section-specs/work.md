# Section Spec: WORK (The Pattern Book)

> Generated by /plan-section. Review and edit before building.

## Product Context

- **Job:** Convert a curious visitor into a convinced one — prove, through concrete work, that Grant can build at enterprise scale alone.
- **Content Hierarchy:**
  1. Garment tag — "THE PATTERN BOOK" — sets the tailoring frame
  2. Section heading — "Systems I've Built" — plainly states the payload
  3. Card I/III — AI Orchestration Platform for Revenue Teams (flagship, most technical depth)
  4. Card II/III — Full-Stack AI Infrastructure — Early-Stage Startup (breadth: frontend to DevOps)
  5. Card III/III — Brand Strategy & Operations System — Premium Consumer Brand (shows the origin: real domain expertise, not just code)
- **Emotional Arc Position:** The visitor has just been struck by the hero. Now they need evidence. This section delivers the "he actually builds things" proof before the decorative Suit Spec blueprint.
- **Tailoring Metaphor:** A pattern book is the master record of every pattern a tailor has cut. Each card is a pattern — a documented, repeatable system. The numbering (I/III, II/III, III/III) is a tailor's notation, not developer convention. The amber rule that sweeps the top border on hover is a basting thread being drawn across the card.

## Design Direction

- **Fabric Texture:** Houndstooth — SVG data URI, inline via `#work::before`, 16px tile, 0.025 opacity. Half-blocks + polygon teeth.
- **Background Layers (back to front):** Houndstooth (`#work::before`, z-index: -1) → Section content
- **Layout:** Single column, full-width cards. No grid. Cards are separated by a `border-top: 1px solid var(--color-divider)`. Max content width 1100px (inherited from `.section-wrapper`).

**Card anatomy (per card, top to bottom):**
- `case-card-number` — absolute, top-right, 0.55rem Outfit, amber 25% opacity (e.g. "I/III")
- `case-card-category` — 0.66rem Outfit 500, uppercase, 0.22em tracking, amber (e.g. "AI ORCHESTRATION")
- `case-card-title` — Syne 600, 1.4rem
- `case-card-desc` — Outfit 400, 0.95rem, muted, max-width 700px, line-height 1.7
- `case-card-metrics` — Outfit 400, 0.78rem, text-faint, line-height 2 — dense spec list separated by amber `MetricDot` (·)
- `case-card-closer` — Outfit italic, 0.88rem, amber-light — one punchy line closing each card

**Hover state:**
- Card lifts `translateY(-2px)`, subtle amber box-shadow (6% opacity)
- Amber rule (`::before`) sweeps full width at top border (0 → 100%, 0.6s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1))

**Animations:**
- Section heading: clip-path curtain reveal (`.clip-reveal` → `.clip-reveal.visible`) on IntersectionObserver
- Garment tag: `.tag-enter` reveal on IntersectionObserver
- Cards: `.fade-in` within `.stagger` parent — stagger triggered by `useScrollReveal` hook
- Card closers: `.closer-fade` — opacity 0 → 1 on IntersectionObserver

**Entrance timing:** Cards stagger with ~80ms between each. The `.stagger` wrapper handles this automatically via the global `useScrollReveal` implementation.

- **Typography:**
  - Garment tag: Outfit 500, 0.68rem, uppercase, tracked — same as global `.garment-tag`
  - Section heading: Syne 700, `clamp(1.8rem, 3.5vw, 2.5rem)` — global `.section-heading`
  - Card category: Outfit 500, 0.66rem, uppercase, 0.22em spacing, amber
  - Card title: Syne 600, 1.4rem
  - Card desc: Outfit 400, 0.95rem, muted, line-height 1.7
  - Card metrics: Outfit 400, 0.78rem, text-faint, line-height 2
  - Card closer: Outfit italic, 0.88rem, amber-light
  - Card number: Outfit, 0.55rem, amber 25% opacity

## Success Criteria

1. Each card's amber rule sweeps visibly across the top border on hover (0 → 100%, smooth, not instant)
2. The metric dots (·) are amber-colored and visually separate spec items without adding vertical space
3. The card number (I/III) is legible but clearly subordinate — reads as a label, not a headline
4. Card closers are italic and amber-light, tonally distinct from the description text
5. The houndstooth texture is visible at the section margins but does not compete with the card text
6. All three cards scroll-reveal in staggered sequence, not all at once
7. The section heading reveals via clip-path (not fade-up) when it enters the viewport
8. Cards pass the AI Slop Test: no rounded corners, no drop shadows on the card body, no icons, no "Read more" CTAs
9. On mobile (≤768px), cards remain single-column and houndstooth opacity reduces to ~50% of desktop value
10. The `case-card-desc` max-width (700px) prevents line lengths from becoming uncomfortably long on wide viewports
11. The ruler-mark SectionDivider above this section renders correctly — horizontally centered, full-width tick marks visible, consistent with dividers elsewhere on the page

## Anti-Patterns

1. Do NOT add a "View Project" or "Case Study" CTA button to each card — these are not project links, they are evidence
2. Do NOT convert cards to a 2 or 3-column grid — the stacked column format is intentional (a pattern book is read top to bottom)
3. Do NOT add category icons or emoji — the amber category label is the visual anchor
4. Do NOT use rounded corners (`border-radius`) on the cards — sharp edges are the brand
5. Do NOT add a card-level drop shadow on the resting state — the hover lift should feel like emergence, not decoration
6. Do NOT reorder or replace Card III — the brand/operations card is the tailoring-to-software bridge
7. Do NOT make the metric list collapsible or hidden — the density is the point (it signals thoroughness)
8. Do NOT use generic fade-up for the heading — the clip-path reveal is the deliberate choice per global animation system

## Do Not Touch

- The `MetricDot` component and its amber-colored dot separator
- The card numbering system (I/III, II/III, III/III) and notation style
- The card content (titles, descriptions, metrics, closers) — changes require explicit approval
- The `.stagger` + `.fade-in` animation pattern — do not replace with GSAP or Framer Motion
- The `useScrollReveal` hook that drives all IntersectionObserver-based reveals
- The houndstooth SVG data URI in `#work::before`
