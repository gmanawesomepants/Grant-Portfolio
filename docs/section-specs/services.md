# Section Spec: SERVICES

> Generated by /plan-section. Review and edit before building.

## Product Context
- **Job:** Convert interest into intent — after the visitor has been impressed (hero, work, suit spec), this section answers "what can he actually do for me?" in concrete, scoped offerings.
- **Content Hierarchy:**
  1. Service titles (scannable — visitor decides relevance in 2 seconds)
  2. Service descriptions (tailoring-laced explanations of each engagement type)
  3. Phase labels MEASURE / CUT / FIT (signals a defined process via tailoring vocabulary)
  4. Note on first card (sets commercial expectation: paid audit, not free consultation)
- **Emotional Arc Position:** After the credential wash of the Ticker. The visitor has absorbed competence passively — now they get the structured offering. This is the "fitting menu" at a Savile Row house: here's what we make, here's how it works.
- **Tailoring Metaphor:** The services are pattern pieces — the Audit, Build, and Retainer are three connected panels of a single garment pattern. The section literally unfolds like a pattern piece being opened on a cutting table. Fold lines between panels use actual stitch types (basting, backstitch) that encode the relationship between phases.

## Design Direction

### Layout — Unfolding Pattern Piece
- Full-width section with `section-wrapper` padding
- Garment tag ("SERVICES") top-left, standard `garment-tag` + `tag-enter` classes
- Section heading: "How I Work" — sits above the pinned area, static
- Three panels in a flex row (`.services-pattern`), connected by fold-line dividers
- Panel 2 (Build) is wider: `flex: 1.3` (~36% vs ~32% for others) — subtle visual anchor
- No gaps between panels — fold lines provide visual separation
- Each panel has an outer `<article>` (GSAP animation target) and inner `.panel-inner` (hover/focus target)

### Scroll-Driven Unfold Animation (Desktop)
- **Engine:** GSAP ScrollTrigger with `pin: true`, `scrub: 0.5`, `end: "+=250%"`
- **Sequence:**
  - Panel 1 fills container at full width initially
  - Scroll 0.1–0.4: Panel 1 shrinks, Panel 2 grows from 0% + rotates in (`rotateY(-90deg)` → `0`)
  - Scroll 0.35: Basting fold line fades in at the joint
  - Scroll 0.4–0.5: Hold — panels 1+2 readable
  - Scroll 0.5–0.8: Panel 3 grows + rotates in
  - Scroll 0.75: Backstitch fold line fades in
  - Scroll 0.85: CTA fades in
  - Scroll 0.85–2.5: Hold — all content readable before unpin
- **Pacing:** 34% animation, 66% reading time
- **Perspective:** 1200px on `.services-pattern`
- Panels 2+3 start with `pointer-events: none`, toggled to `auto` after unfold settles
- Lives in `src/animations/servicesUnfold.ts` with init/cleanup exports

### Mobile (≤768px)
- GSAP never initializes (early return on `window.innerWidth <= 768`)
- Panels stack vertically, fold lines become horizontal dividers
- IntersectionObserver drives `.service-panel.visible` fade-in entrance
- `[style]` nuclear reset catches any leaked GSAP inline styles on desktop→mobile resize

### Fabric Texture
- **Sharkskin** — micro diagonal cross-hatch via SVG `data:` URI
- Applied via `#services::before` pseudo-element, full-viewport width, `z-index: -1`
- Section has `isolation: isolate` for stacking context

### Background Layers
1. Page `--color-bg` (#080808) — inherited
2. Sharkskin SVG texture at near-invisible opacity (::before)

### Fold Lines (Vertical Stitch Patterns)
Between panels, structural fold creases using the same stitch vocabulary:

| Fold Line | Position | Width | Pattern | Character |
|-----------|----------|-------|---------|-----------|
| Basting | Between Audit & Build | 1px | 10px dash / 22px gap, vertical | Temporary hold — these phases connect loosely |
| Backstitch | Between Build & Retainer | 3px (double row) | 8px dash / 3px gap ×2 rows, vertical | Structural bond — the ongoing relationship |

Fold lines are `opacity: 0` initially, GSAP reveals them as panels unfold.
On mobile, fold lines rotate to horizontal dividers at `opacity: 0.3`.

### Panel Hover States
- Hover/focus on `.panel-inner` (not outer article — avoids GSAP transform conflict)

- Phase label opacity increases from 0.45 to 0.75 on hover
- First panel has subtle `border-left: 1px solid var(--color-amber-dim)` — pattern piece edge

## Success Criteria
1. Section pins on scroll entry and three panels unfold sequentially — Panel 1 fills container initially, Panels 2+3 grow + rotate in as visitor scrolls
2. Fold lines appear at panel joints: basting (loose vertical dashes) between Audit/Build, backstitch (dense double-row) between Build/Retainer
3. Panel 2 (Build) is visually wider (~36% vs ~32%) — subtle asymmetric anchor
4. 66% of total scroll distance is hold/reading time — animation is front-loaded, reading is back-loaded
5. CTA "Request a Fitting →" fades in after all panels are open, visible while section is still pinned
6. Hover on `.panel-inner` produces subtle lift (-2px) + amber glow — no conflict with GSAP transforms on outer panel
7. Phase labels MEASURE / CUT / FIT appear on each panel — Outfit 0.68rem, amber at 0.45 opacity
8. Mobile (≤768px): no pinning, no unfold — panels stack vertically with horizontal fold-line dividers, IntersectionObserver drives fade-in
9. Reduced motion: all panels visible at final width immediately, fold lines visible, no transforms
10. Body text max-width is 540px — prevents unreadable line lengths
11. Sharkskin fabric texture present via `#services::before`
12. Clip-reveal on heading works independently of the pinned unfold
13. Tailoring language is woven into descriptions naturally
14. Keyboard focus-visible on `.panel-inner` mirrors hover state
15. `aria-hidden` on fold lines, descriptive `aria-label` on `.panel-inner` elements
16. Panels 2+3 have `pointer-events: none` during unfold, toggled to `auto` after settling — no hover on mid-rotation panels
17. Final unfolded state reads as one connected object with three zones, not three separate cards

## Anti-Patterns
1. No card backgrounds, rounded corners, or drop shadows at rest
2. No icons or illustrations on panels
3. No "Learn More" buttons or CTAs on individual panels
4. No skill bars, percentage meters, or tech logos
5. No gradient borders or animated borders
6. No card flip effects
7. No pricing information
8. No 3D perspective flips or bouncy easing — the unfold should feel physical and deliberate, like hands opening a folded pattern piece
9. No scroll jacking — animation completes briskly, majority of pinned time is for reading

## Do Not Touch
- The three-service structure (Audit → Build → Retainer) and their ordering
- The tailoring language in service descriptions
- The fold-line stitch patterns (basting, backstitch) and their structural meaning
- The phase labels (MEASURE, CUT, FIT) and their mapping to the thread system vocabulary
- The wrapper approach: outer `<article>` for GSAP, inner `.panel-inner` for hover/focus
- The scroll pacing ratio (34% animation / 66% reading)
- The sharkskin fabric texture SVG and its opacity
- The thread system's "FIT" mapping to this section in `src/animations/thread.ts`
